How one person does the work of a team

  1. Claude Code writes alongside me

    Claude Code is an AI coding partner that runs on my Linux workstation. I decide what your app needs; it helps write, test and fix the code. Work that used to need a team fits into one person's week.

  2. Cloudflare Workers runs your app

    Your app is deployed to Cloudflare's network in more than 330 cities. There is no server to babysit, HTTPS is on by default, and pages load fast on any phone.

  3. Your customers just open a link

    No app store and no download. It works in the browser on any iPhone or Android, in English and Spanish when you need both.
